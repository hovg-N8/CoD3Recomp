What v36 does:
 1. Host: a joining copy that sends a CWP1 that is not 44 bytes, or a 28-byte CWG1, is an older build. Logged once
    ("coop: players: the joining copy runs an older co-op build ...: both PCs need the same CoD3.exe") and an
    on-screen notice every 30 s. (The joiner already warned about an older host, CWS1, in v35.)
 2. Host: while in a mission and alive, CWF1 (his film is over) with his count is sent every second. A joining copy
    that starts again after the host's death or retry leaves its film at once. A film at a mission's start is not
    affected: the host sends nothing until his own player is in.
 3. Joiner: after 15 s of film the joining player's own A ends it (logged "coop: film: skipped here (no word from
    the host after N s)"). A safety net, so a lost CWF1 can never leave him stuck again. The notice says so.
 4. DIAGNOSTIC, off unless COD3_COOP_SHOTLOG=1: the weapon time (+52), buttons (+1352), pad trigger, +48 and +56
    whenever one changes ("coop: shotlog").

Shot detection checked (with 4), not changed: solo Forest, 0.05-0.4 s trigger taps: every shot counted (0->300).
Solo Chambois with taps, aiming plus taps, and 1.5 s holds: 116 of 116 shots counted (0->150). The only rises not
counted are 500/600/850/1000 (reload steps), as intended.
