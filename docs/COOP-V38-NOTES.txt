What v38 does:
 1. Dropped weapons and grenades are never sent, paired or moved (each copy's own physics). (a)
 2. CWI1 (20 bytes: key, place): a dropped weapon picked up or cleared away in one copy is deleted in the
    other (its own of that key nearest the place, within 128 units; the scripts' delete). Both ways.
 3. Grenades, CWG3 (56 bytes): sent the frame a grenade appears, with its own trajectory (entity +32
    where it was let go, +44 its speed; found by dumping a thrown grenade). The other copy throws one
    (magicgrenade, from that same place; 64/128 higher targets if no arc) and writes the same +32/+44
    into it: the same arc, off the same walls. The CWG2 of its landing (same id) follows: the other
    copy's grenade, once it lies still too, is moved there if 80+ units off (bodies, not the same in the
    two copies, can bounce it). An id thrown early is never thrown again. A CWG2 alone (CWG3 lost) is
    thrown as before. Grenade ids now start from the clock (not 1 again after a relaunch). (b)
 4. Soldiers' shots, CWA1: sub_823ED9B8 (actor shoot; only the scripts' shoot builtins sub_824B8FF8 /
    sub_824B9088 call it, the animscripts' fire included) is hooked. Host: each soldier's shot is sent
    (his clock, entity number) every 50 ms. Joiner: the soldier paired with him fires at the host's time
    less the world's display delay; the joiner's paired soldiers and the host's body do not fire on their
    own while the host's world arrives (they do again if it stops for 1 s). Host: the joiner's stand-in
    no longer fires on its own (only when the joining player does). (g)
 5. Facing by the game's orientmode "face angle" (sub_824ABE18 writes actor +372 = 1, +376/+388 a
    constant at 0x82062C54, +380/+384 the yaw): the players' bodies always face where that player looks
    (strafing/backpedalling no longer runs sideways); a soldier standing still faces where the host's
    does; moving, his own facing is given back.
 6. Stuck: one put in place 5 times in 2 s is not driven again; a stuck player body / stand-in is hidden
    and replaced, a stuck soldier's pair released. (c)
 7. A host soldier with no partner for 1.5 s: first one of the joiner's own unpaired "_reg" soldiers is
    taken for him (his kind, else his side), put at his place and shown; only then the spawners: 3 tries
    of his kind, 3 of his side (another kind). The joiner's own unpaired soldiers are no longer deleted
    (the scripts' delete left them alive, unmovable, and filling the game's soldiers): hidden, silent
    and kept below the level until one is taken for a host soldier. (f)
 8. MakeBody (the players' bodies) takes each friendly spawner in turn, not always the first, and logs
    failures. "coop: players: no body for the host after N s: ..." says once a mission why there is
    none (player record, host's place, entries, tries). (d: cause still to be seen in a log.)
 9. The joiner reads the film port every frame (stale CWF1s dropped; logged 5 times at most). (e)
10. Diagnostics: COD3_COOP_NADESHOT=1 (grenades thrown for the other copy followed to where they go off,
    pictures at +0.3 s and +1 s), COD3_COOP_SHOTLOG=1 (weapon time, buttons, trigger).
