Co-op v35 (Team Friend, 2026-10-20), made on Team Justin's 0.5.313 source. Answers co-op test 6.
Only CoD3Host/coop.cpp changed (campaign program only, #ifndef COD3_MP; nothing in CoD3MP).
Built here as CoD3.exe from 0.5.313 + this coop.cpp; one-PC test (two copies, Forest) results are in
COOP-V35-ONE-PC-TEST.txt. UNTESTED between two PCs. Both PCs need the same CoD3.exe: the messages
changed (CWS2 / CWG2 / 44-byte CWP1); an older host is reported on the joining screen.

What the joining player saw in test 6 and earlier: soldiers jittering, cartwheeling in place, running
sideways, popping up far away; few of the host's grenades or smoke; few weapon drops.

Causes found (from test 6's host log and the joiner's own coop-join-play.log of Oct 18):
 a. The host's whole world went as ONE UDP message of ~340 entities (12 KB, up to 32 KB) 20 times a
    second. Tailscale's packets are 1280 bytes, so each message was 10+ IP fragments; losing any one
    lost the whole update. Then the next one jumped.
 b. Places were timed by when they ARRIVED, so internet jitter became speed jitter.
 c. The joiner never forgot entities the host had deleted (its list sat at 348 for minutes), so
    their partners kept being pulled to old places.
 d. A soldier was slid toward the host's place every frame while his own legs (setgoalpos) also
    walked him, and his body was turned to the host's facing whatever way his legs went: the two
    fought (jitter) and he ran sideways.
 e. When a host soldier died and his entity lost its actor, it was sent as an ordinary moving thing.
    The joiner's soldier paired with it was NOT killed (the kill path needed the actor flag) and got
    the corpse's lying-down angles every frame: the cartwheels. No death there = no weapon dropped.
 f. Soldiers were paired with the nearest of their class at ANY distance (worst 20000+ units in the
    joiner's log), then snapped there: the "random spawning".
 g. Only the players' grenades were sent; the joiner's soldiers threw their own (not the host's).
 h. magicgrenade (sub_824B27A8) fails when its three arc tries (sub_823D6DB0, sub_823D6EB0,
    sub_823D85B8) find no arc from the hand: test 6's "NOT thrown". It reads the weapon from actor
    +2564 (checked in the code; the builtins table's +2504 is the scripts' grenadeweapon field).
 i. The joiner's smoke was thrown from inside his stand-in on the host and hurt him (586/449/821).

What v35 does:
 1. CWS2 world parts: header "CWS2", sequence u32, host clock f64 (seconds), part u16, parts u16;
    then up to 30 SyncEntry (36 bytes; same layout as before) = 1100 bytes, one packet, each part
    useful alone. Only soldiers, the players, the stand-ins and things that have moved are sent
    (30-60 entries instead of 340). (a, c)
 2. NetClock + Track: the receiver learns the clock offset (min-tracking, jitter measured) and
    shows each thing at sender time "now - offset - delay" (delay 0.1 s + 1.5 x jitter, at most
    +0.25; COD3_COOP_DELAY sets the base), interpolating between real updates and predicting ahead
    from the last speed for up to 0.2 s when an update is late (no vertical prediction). (b)
 3. An entry not heard for 1 s is dropped on the joiner (only while updates are arriving). (c)
 4. DriveActor (soldiers, and both players' bodies): his own legs walk him (setgoalpos, 0.35 s
    ahead of the host's movement, renewed every 0.2 s if it moved 16 units, goal radius 24 moving /
    12 standing). He is only pushed when off by more than a dead zone (32 units; a player's body
    16), at a speed that grows with the error (capped 700/s), and put there outright only past
    COD3_COOP_SNAP (320). A soldier's angles are NEVER written (except at a snap: yaw only); a
    player's body turns to the player's view yaw only while standing still. (d)
    DriveThing (doors, gates, vehicles): placed exactly at the buffered place and angles.
 5. Deaths: the host remembers which entities were soldiers; one that loses its actor is still sent
    as a soldier with health 0. The joiner kills its partner (dodamage, as before: he falls and drops
    his weapon there) and never moves a body or a non-soldier into a soldier again. Things of class
    'actor_*' are never moved as things. (e)
 6. Pairing: a living host soldier pairs only with a LIVING soldier here within 800 units. A pair
    whose soldier died here while the host's lives is let go. A living host soldier with no partner
    for 1.5 s gets a new one: dospawn (sub_824B9118) from the nearest spawner (type 12) whose
    type+classname key matches, put at the host's place (4 a second at most, 3 tries each). (f)
    The story's own soldiers (no "_reg" in the class: Dixon, the medic, Guzzo, Huxley) pair at
    any distance and are never hidden or removed. The joiner's own unpaired "_reg" soldiers are
    hidden after 0.75 s (hide flag 0x400 of entity +632, shown again if paired); at 4 s the
    scripts' delete is tried once (the one-PC test: it leaves a LIVING soldier be), then he is
    kept below the level (HideBelow) each second until paired again.
 7. Grenades, CWG2 (32 bytes): id u32 (sent twice, taken once), thrower u16 (the sender's entity
    number; 1 = the sender's player), kind u16 (1 frag, 2 smoke), landing place, weapon u32, fuse
    f32. The host sends EVERY grenade (thrower = nearest soldier/player within 150 units when it
    appears); the joiner only its player's. A grenade must have flown 40 units (one lying smoke was
    re-sent 3 times in test 6). The joiner's soldiers keep no grenades of their own (actor +2576
    grenadeammo = 0 each 0.5 s; one is lent for a remote throw). Grenades thrown for the other copy
    are never sent back. RemoteGrenade tries 4 release points: hand height, higher, halfway up, and
    last straight above the landing place. (g, h)
 8. The host's stand-in for the joiner takes no damage from his own grenade until its fuse + 1 s
    (logged "the stand-in's own grenade hit him for N: not sent"), and never more than 250 at once. (i)
 9. Stances on bodies change only after holding 0.25 s (test 6's "->1" then "->4" pairs).
10. Stand-in choice: MakeBody tried for a minute (30 tries, was 10) before borrowing; borrowing any
    non-"_reg" ally (Dixon in test 6) is off unless COD3_COOP_ANYALLY=1.
11. CWP1 (joiner -> host) now 44 bytes: + the joiner's clock (f64) for the same buffer on the host.
    The host still takes 28/32/36-byte ones.
12. COD3_COOP_APPLY is on by default for the joiner now (=0 turns it off); the old "=1" slide mode
    and COD3_COOP_SNAPPY are gone.
13. Sprint (SprintPoll, campaign only): made to play like multiplayer's. The campaign program has no
    sprint of its own (multiplayer's start sub_8248E970 and stamina sub_82495980, ps +44 flags
    0x10000/0x20000, stamina +1168, have no counterpart in default.xex), so it is still g_speed:
    tap to run (toggle_sprint as before; firing, aiming, crouch, prone or letting go of forward ends
    it), speed rises to 1.5x over 0.25 s (it jumped at once), a run lasts 4 s (COD3_SPRINT_SECONDS),
    run dry = out of breath 1.5 s then back from a quarter; stopped yourself = refills after 0.75 s,
    full from empty in 3 s (the numbers of mp_fixes.cpp's quick sprint recovery). No lowered-gun
    sprint animation: the campaign has none to play.

14. One-PC testing (LOCAL-COOP-TEST.bat in this package): COD3_SPINWAIT=0 for both copies and
    the joiner started 40 s after the host. On an 8-thread PC two copies that spin on their waits
    starved each other to 0 fps at the menu (black screens), and two started at once stalled the
    first in its opening film (a GPU wait). Not a two-PC problem; no code change for it.

New log lines to grep: "coop: world at" (now with clock delay/jitter and "put in place outright"
counts), "coop: move:", "made one (from spawner", "has no partner on the host: let go", "coop: body:
N threw the other copy's ... (try N)", "coop: world: a soldier's grenade", "sprint:".
