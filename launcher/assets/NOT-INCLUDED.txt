Not in this repository (put them here yourself before running BUILD.ps1):

- battle-ambience.wav, normandy-score.wav, field-radio.wav, frontline-mix.wav:
  the launcher's audio. frontline-mix.wav contains a supplied commercial
  recording and field-radio.wav supplied radio audio, so none of the audio
  is shared here. tools/make_ambience.py, make_score.py, mix_field_radio.py
  and Make-FieldRadio.ps1 show how they were made.
- treyarch-logo.png: Treyarch's logo (credits screen).

The background art and the JARC emblem in this folder are the launcher's own.
